# The Undiscovered Country Review

*Technology, discovery and practice for a life of wonder, connection and meaning.*

The Undiscovered Country Review is a Monday newsletter from the Undiscovered Country Institute (UCI). Each edition gathers hopeful, evidence-backed work on technology and human flourishing: AI and discovery, robots that serve people, the science of connection, health, and the living world. Every issue opens with a moment of wonder and closes with a practice to try and a look at life lived offline.

This repository is the public archive of published editions.

## How the review is made

**This review is AI-produced.** Each edition, three AI systems — **Claude**, **ChatGPT** and **Grok** — research in parallel, merge their findings, and fact-check one another against the original sources. **Claude** makes the final selection and writes the issue. The review itself contains no human-written text.

**Jonathan Trouern-Trend**, founder of the Undiscovered Country Institute, reads every issue before it is published and adds his own commentary when he posts it on [undiscovered-country.ghost.io](https://undiscovered-country.ghost.io). The editions in this repository are the review text only; his commentary lives on the Ghost site.

## The Diamond Constitution

The review is one small, public test of UCI's [**Diamond Constitution**](https://github.com/Jtrouern/diamond-constitution), an open draft of twelve principles for how AI should serve human flourishing. The review tries to live up to it in practice: every claim is sourced and caveated (Article II), a person stands behind each issue (Article V), errors get corrected when readers point them out (Article X), the aim is more curiosity rather than more screen time (Article IV), and each issue closes with the case for time no machine records (Article XII).

## Corrections

Found an error, a broken link, or a claim that doesn't match its source? Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Jtrouern/UCI_Review/issues) on this repository. Corrections are made in the edition files here and noted in the issue that raised them.
